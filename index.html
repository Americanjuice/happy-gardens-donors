Our Story

Lydia's story

Lydia Awuor Onyango lost her mother when she was five and was later raised by her grandparents. As a child she took herself and her younger sister to school without books, pencils or uniforms. With the help of teachers, well-wishers and donors, she finished her education through college.

After 15 years teaching in Nairobi's slum settlements, she saw during COVID-19 how vulnerable children fell behind while others learned online. She began teaching children in her home with a cup of porridge, all she could afford. In 2021 she founded Happy Gardens Community School.

Today the school teaches children from Playgroup to Grade 2 and gives them breakfast, lunch and snacks, often the only meals they get. Lydia's goal is to reach at least 300 children and open more branches across Kenya.

“Feed a child, feed a community, and fuel them to school.” — Lydia Awuor Onyango

What We Do

Education, meals and care

Education for all

Playgroup, Primary One, Primary Two, Grade One and Grade Two. Every child is welcome, whatever their background or ability to pay fees.

Feeding program

Breakfast, lunch and snacks fight malnutrition. Some children come to school specifically for the meal. When friends sponsor food, Saturday meal days continue in the holidays.

Reading and writing

Children learn in English and Swahili. From January 2027, Happy Gardens begins a free, paper-based writing pilot with Raven Ink to build comprehension.
